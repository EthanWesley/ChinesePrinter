Privacy Policy — ChinesePrinter Sidebar

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Overview

ChinesePrinter Sidebar (the “Extension”) is a browser extension that opens a Chinese text-input (typewriter) interface for the local device associated with the web page you are currently viewing. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Extension accesses and how that information is handled.

2. Information the Extension Accesses

  • Current tab URL (hostname only). When you click the Extension icon, the Extension reads the URL of the currently active tab in order to determine the hostname (IP address or domain) of the device whose typewriter page should be opened. This value is used only to construct a local address of the form http://<hostname>:8848/ and to display it in the side panel.

3. How the Information Is Used and Stored

  • All processing occurs locally within your browser.
  • The Extension does not store, log, or retain the current tab URL or any other data after the side panel is closed.
  • The Extension does not transmit any data to the developer, to any server operated by the developer, or to any third party.
  • The Extension contains no analytics, tracking, advertising, or telemetry components.

4. Network Requests

The only network request made is from your browser to the device at http://<hostname>:8848/ — that is, the device on your own network that you are already viewing. This request goes directly from your browser to your local device and does not pass through any developer-operated server.

5. Permissions and Their Purpose

  • activeTab / tabs — used to read the hostname of the active tab and to detect tab switches so the panel can reload the correct device page.
  • sidePanel — used to display the typewriter interface in the browser’s side panel.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties. We do not collect personal data.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Extension does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
